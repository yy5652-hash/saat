**Title:** Saat: hourly proof for clean energy (IEEE ClimateChain Hackathon 2026)

**Visibility:** Unlisted · not made for kids

**Description:**

Saat makes the hour the unit of proof for clean electricity: AI-verified hourly certificates, traded peer to peer, retired on-chain only against same-hour load.

• On Türkiye's real 2025 grid (EPİAŞ data), "100% renewable" covers 72% of electricity in the same hour.
• An AI verifier, tested on a real 4.7 MW solar plant, blocks fake energy that a capacity check lets through.
• The demo day runs live on Ethereum Sepolia: the hotel's hourly score, computed on-chain, is 84.6%.
